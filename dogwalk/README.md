# Heel, Biscuit!

A city dog-walking game set in golden-hour autumn light — an editorial-fashion
promenade through Ashbourne Park, starring Biscuit, a wire-haired Jack Russell
terrier.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. No build step, no dependencies.

## How to play

- **↑ / ↓** (or W/S, or swipe on touch) — change path on the promenade
- **Space** (or tap) — whistle; when a squirrel appears, whistle before the
  ring closes or Biscuit will drag you toward it
- **P** — pause, **M** — toggle sound

Dodge puddles, fallen branches, planters, and oncoming poodle-walkers.
Collect golden leaves and biscuits — biscuits lift Biscuit's spirits, and
high spirits multiply your score up to ×3. Three collisions end the walk.
