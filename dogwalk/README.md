# Heel, Biscuit!

A city dog-walking game set in golden-hour autumn light — an editorial-fashion
promenade through Ashbourne Park, starring Biscuit, a wire-haired Jack Russell
terrier. One-touch, momentum-driven strolling over rolling hills, in the
spirit of Alto's Adventure.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. No build step, no dependencies.

## How to play

- **Space** (or ↑/W, click, tap) — leap; hold for a longer leap, tap for a hop
- When a squirrel appears and the ring is counting down, the same button
  **whistles** — miss it and Biscuit hauls you off your stride
- **P** — pause, **M** — toggle sound

Speed builds downhill and bleeds uphill. Land with the slope for a *flow*
boost; long leaps earn *hang time*. Jump the puddles, branches, hydrants,
and planters. Golden leaves score; biscuits lift Biscuit's spirits, and high
spirits multiply everything up to ×3. Three collisions end the walk, while
the evening slides from golden hour into lamplight around you.
